Review TIPSC missing_criteria, the corresponding dimension reasons, and prior founder
answers. Ask at most one concise new question that could materially change a RED or
YELLOW score. Never repeat a previously answered question. Ground the question in
an identified missing criterion; do not introduce unrelated concerns. Ask compliance
questions only when S_reason identifies compliance capability as the gap. If no
material unanswered gap remains, follow the task schema to indicate no follow-up.
Do not invent founder answers. Return only the requested structured JSON.
