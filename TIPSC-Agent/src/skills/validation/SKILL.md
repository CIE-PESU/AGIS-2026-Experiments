---
name: validation
description: Validate founder assumptions using external evidence.
---

Investigate only the assumptions explicitly supplied by the founder. Use search tools
when available. Record what was searched, what was found, and the supporting sources.
Classify each assumption as CONFIRMED, UNCONFIRMED, or CONTRADICTED. Missing evidence
means UNCONFIRMED, not false. Never invent research, competitors, or market facts.
Include a competitor landscape and market notes for the stated geography and sector.
Distinguish founder claims from independently verified evidence. If research fails or
times out, explicitly report the limitation and leave unsupported claims unconfirmed.
